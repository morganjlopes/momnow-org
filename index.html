Questions

We've got you.

Who's behind MomNow

MomNow.org is an independent nonprofit with an independent board. Our technology partner, SupportNow, has helped families raise support through hard seasons. [Legal status / fiscal sponsor]

Is it really free?

Yes. Families never pay a fee or a share of their savings. Donors fund our work.

Who is this for?

Families facing financial hardship because of illness, disability, injury or loss. You don't need a fundraiser or a SupportNow account.

Will you pay my whole bill?

We work to lower your bills as far as possible and set up an interest-free plan for what's left. Grants can help with the remaining balance when funds allow, but we can't promise to cover every bill.

Is my information safe?

Your medical and financial details are used only to work on your bills, shared only with your permission, and never sold.

Do you give legal advice?

No. If a bill involves a lawsuit, we'll point you to free legal help.
